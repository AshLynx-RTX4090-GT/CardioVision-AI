Company Description

CardioVision AI is engineered by LYNX Cardio Hospitals & Care Berlin. Our core mission is bridging the gap between non-invasive outpatient biometric telemetry and high-precision coronary artery stenosis detection.

By synthesizing machine learning trained on gold-standard coronary angiography data with real-time wearable sensor dynamics, CardioVision provides patients and cardiologists with actionable, explainable (SHAP) physiological insights prior to invasive catheterization.

Privacy Policy, Data Governance & Terms of Service

1. Biometric Data Protection & GDPR Compliance

All continuous ECG, pulse, blood pressure, and demographic values collected via the CardioTag Sensor or synchronized through Google Fit / Apple Health are protected under GDPR Article 9 (Special Category Health Data) and international HIPAA equivalents. End-to-end 256-bit AES encryption is enforced during transmission and at rest. Data is strictly processed for the authenticated patient and is never monetized or shared with third-party advertisers.

2. Terms of Clinical Decision Support

CardioVision AI predictions and 3D coronary visualizations are designed exclusively for clinical decision support, risk triage, and educational exploration. Predictions do not constitute emergency medical advice nor do they replace invasive coronary angiography, fractional flow reserve (FFR), or computed tomography coronary angiography (CTCA). Patients experiencing acute chest pain or myocardial infarction symptoms must immediately dial emergency medical services.
